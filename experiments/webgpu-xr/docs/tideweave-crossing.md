## Completion sound

The D-Dorian target motif stays D–F–B–A. `bridgeScore.ts` defines a short
completion answer: A4 acknowledges the final target, then D3/D4/D5 settle the
phrase. `finishTrace(midi, BRIDGE_RESOLUTION)` uses the existing shared audio
owner and its volume, stop, mute and disposal rules. Completion sound is earned
by the real trace; page load, saved completion and arbitrary later-note input
must not trigger it. There is no microphone, vocal-technique judgement or new
scoring system.
