# Irchiinnuss musical worlds

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Adults who enjoy music and are beginners at singing. The first playable build
is explored on a desktop with a mouse, touch or keyboard. A Quest version is a
future validation step; headset interaction and performance remain untested.

## Product Purpose

Be an explorer in Irchiinnuss: hear a world, shape its musical form, and record
the encounter in an Almanac. A live fractal orb gives each world a visible
identity. Music and direct interaction make that identity something to explore.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Two playable worlds lead to earned Almanac pages. Glasswake asks the player
  to hold and release C, E and G to open three glass seals. Tideweave asks the
  player to trace a heard D, F, B, A melody to grow a crossing.
- Collecting both pages makes a shared musical passage and temple reveal
  available. The temple interior is not playable.
- The seven-constellation atlas includes five future world previews. Those
  previews do not yet have playable encounters or completed musical content.
- The Almanac lets the player select a world, inspect its orb and replay its
  motif. Earned details record completed encounters and remain after replay.
- Progress is stored in the current browser when storage is available. The
  interface must disclose temporary or protected saves accurately.
- The current encounters use guided instruments. They do not record a
  microphone, assess singing technique, assign a singing grade, or evaluate
  breathiness. Voice learning remains a longer-term direction.
- Keep SolidJS and the existing WebGPU/TypeGPU rendering path. Desktop proof
  does not establish WebXR, hand-tracking or physical Quest acceptance.

## Product Principles

- Let the fractal, musical response and earned object carry the experience.
- Prefer personal progress and forgiving retries. Replaying keeps earned pages.
- Make the two worlds distinct while retaining a coherent book and atlas.
- Preserve useful mouse, touch and keyboard controls and reduced-motion behavior.
- Separate playable content, future proposals and technical validation claims.

## Brand Commitments

Lumen Apeiron is the product; Irchiinnuss is the fictional universe. The player
is an explorer, with a temple teaser connecting the first two regions. Further
book-specific story details need the owner's supplied synopsis.

Use concrete, direct language. Apply the shared product-voice skill. Avoid
decorative promises, unearned capability claims and emojis in the interface.

## Evidence on Hand

The experiment contains Blender-authored book and observatory assets, two
playable desktop encounters, generated live fractal specimens, original musical
motifs, earned-page persistence and a temple ending. Source assets live in
`assets-source/`; runtime models live in `public/models/`. Detailed research,
concepts and review evidence live in the owner's local `personal/lumen-meta`
documentation directory. Hardware acceptance remains separate from those
desktop demonstrations.
