# Atlas specimen posters

The five WebP files are captured from this app's `FlameOrb` renderer and the
matching recipes in `orbPresets.ts`, using their default camera positions.
Captured 2026-09-24 in headed Chrome on an AMD RDNA 4 hardware WebGPU adapter.
They are not AI-generated concept images. The frame contains only the rendered
specimen against black; orbital guides, labels and other worlds are hidden.

The small satellites and the unavailable-WebGPU view use these static images.
The selected world replaces its poster after its first detailed live pass.
Regenerate the matching poster when changing a preset's geometry or palette.
The original renderer is stochastic, so later captures can vary slightly.

## Cosmic environment

`cosmic-nebula.webp` is a fictional nebula environment generated with ChatGPT's
built-in image-generation tool on 2026-09-24 for this atlas. The 1672×941 PNG
master is saved in the personal research pack as `concepts/07-cosmic-backdrop.png`;
the shipped WebP is a quality-88 conversion with unchanged dimensions. It is
decorative background art, not a scientific sky map or runtime flame render.
No Meshy credits or third-party model assets were used for this environment.

Prompt: landscape 16:9 deep-space environment for a fullscreen fractal atlas;
blue-black voids, a diagonal river of silver/cyan starlight, restrained amber
gas and violet undertones, fine varied stars and intricate gaseous filaments.
Astrophotography texture with cinematic environmental art; keep the central
50% and lower left dark for interactive objects and UI, detailed galaxy at
upper right and far diagonal edges. No UI, letters, logos, planets, large
spheres, hands, ships, people, horizon, ground or watermark. The complete
generation prompt is preserved in the personal research pack.
